Polygon Geometry

Polygons are closed 2D shapes with straight sides. They are classified by their interior angles:

  • Convex: All interior angles less than 180°. No dents.
  • Concave: At least one interior angle greater than 180°. Has an inward dent.
  • Complex: Edges cross each other. Not a simple polygon.
